# Phase: design

Decide how the feature works before deciding how it is built. Describe the
behaviour a person sees and the choices behind it. No code yet.

Cover:
- What the person does and what they see, step by step (the happy path and
  the two or three ways it goes wrong).
- The decisions made and the alternatives rejected, in one line each.
- What is explicitly out of scope.

Keep it to the line cap. Run `hi phase done` when finished.

# Design

## Behaviour

## Decisions

## Out of scope
